Maya Universe

A complete map of Maya's story across five simulations – characters, learning objectives, and everything IDs need to build what comes next.

7simulations 21scenarios 510videos total ~20 minper simulation

Maya Universe is a series of simulations for early-career professionals navigating job searching, interviews, and starting work. Each simulation puts the learner in a real moment from Maya's story — a career decision, a networking call, an interview, a first day. This map documents the narrative arc, the characters, the learning objectives, and the production rules that hold the universe together. Use it to understand what exists, where it fits, and how to build what comes next.

01 · Narrative timeline – canonical story order
SIM 01 Maya's Dilemma ✓ Complete

Maya has a stable job that no longer feels like hers. She's drawn to something more creative – but she can't tell whether the pull is genuine or just a reaction to everyone else's expectations. Learner plays John, her mentor and former colleague.

Learner plays: John (mentor) Characters: Maya, John, Alex
S1 Is This Really What I Want? – Maya interrogates whether her motivation is genuine or reactive. John helps her separate her own thinking from outside pressure.
S2 What's It Actually Like? – Maya has romanticized creative work. John helps her challenge her assumptions and look at realistic trade-offs.
S3 What Do I Do Next? – Maya knows what she wants but freezes when she tries to plan her whole future. John helps her focus on the next step, not the entire path.
SIM 02 Maya's Network ✓ Complete

Maya has applied to roles and heard nothing. She needs to move beyond job boards. Learner plays Maya – navigating three conversations that each require a different kind of confidence.

Learner plays: Maya Characters: Maya, Adrian, Claire, Tom, Alex
S1 What Should I Do Next? – Adrian helps Maya see that waiting isn't a strategy and that networking is how you access the hidden job market.
S2 The Call With Claire – Maya reaches out to an old acquaintance now working in creative. She needs to ask for a contact without making it awkward.
S3 The Call With Tom – Maya has 15 minutes with Tom, a senior industry contact. She needs to ask the right questions and close the conversation professionally.
SIM 03 Maya's Unexpected Call ✓ Complete

Maya is on a busy street when a recruiter calls out of the blue about a Content and Design Associate role at Fold Studio. Learner plays Maya – handling the call, selling herself without her CV in hand, and making her case for salary flexibility.

Learner plays: Maya Characters: Maya, Cara, Alex S1: Audio only
S1 The Call Comes In – Audio only. Maya on a noisy street. How she answers and whether she manages the environment without losing the opportunity.
S2 Sell Yourself – Video call via Teams. Cara has Maya's CV; Maya doesn't. She needs to speak to her own background confidently without the safety net of having it in front of her.
S3 Make Your Case – Maya gives a problem-solving example and handles a salary flexibility question to close the call well.
SIM 04 Maya's Virtual Interview ✓ Complete

Maya is at home for a video call interview with Jordan Ellis at Fold Studio. The call doesn't go smoothly — there's unexpected background noise, audio drops, a screen share that doesn't come through, and a question she wasn't prepared for. Learner plays Maya.

Learner plays: Maya Characters: Maya, Jordan, Alex Fold Studio Video call
S1 Right From the Start – Unexpected background noise at the start of the call. Then Jordan's audio drops mid-answer and asks Maya to repeat herself.
S2 Something's Not Working on Their End – Jordan's audio cuts out mid-question. Then a screen share fails to come through on Maya's end.
S3 Hold Your Ground – An unexpected question with no obvious right answer. Then Jordan goes silent and the screen freezes.
SIM 05 Maya's Panel Interview ✓ Complete

Maya is at Fold Studio for a panel interview. Learner plays Maya – navigating an introduction with the talent lead, a deeper conversation with the creative director, and an unexpected twist in the final scenario.

Learner plays: Maya Characters: Maya, Diane, Rowan, Kit, Alex Fold Studio
S1 First Impression – Diane opens with "tell me about yourself" and pushes on where else Maya is applying. Specificity and honesty vs. generic answers.
S2 Show Your Work – Rowan asks for a concrete example under pressure. STAR method applied to a real creative brief.
S3 The Unexpected – Kit (senior partner) enters unexpectedly. Maya's role in the room shifts. She handles closing questions and reads the dynamic correctly.
SIM 06 Maya's Night Before ✓ Complete

Maya got the job. Tomorrow is her first day at Fold Studio. Tonight, Javi shows up and finds her getting ready to go out. Learner plays Javi – helping Maya make smart decisions about preparation, rest, and managing the life changes that come with starting something new.

Learner plays: Javi Characters: Maya, Javi, Alex
S1 One More Night – Maya wants to go out before her first day. Javi helps her see why rest and preparation matter.
S2 Did You Even Read This? – Maya has skimmed the information pack. Javi pushes her to actually engage with what's in it.
S3 The First Week – Maya hasn't sorted practical things for tomorrow. Javi helps her get ready – practically and mentally.
SIM 07 Maya's First Day ✓ Complete

Maya's first day at Fold Studio. Luca, her manager, walks her through the role. Noa, the most senior team member, briefs her on client processes. By mid-afternoon Maya has made her first mistake. Learner plays Maya.

Learner plays: Maya Characters: Maya, Luca, Noa, Alex Fold Studio
S1 First Morning – Luca mentions the studio uniform has wool in it. Maya has an allergy she forgot to flag. Then Luca asks her to use a system she's never seen before.
S2 Into the Team – Noa walks Maya through the client briefing process. Maya has relevant experience but has to figure out how to be present without being dismissive.
S3 First Mistake – Maya has sent a draft brief to the wrong person. She has to tell Luca. Then Luca changes the plan unexpectedly.

03 · Cross-universe character notes

These characters appear in both Maya Universe and Ellie Universe. This section documents their consistency — and flags anything IDs should align on before writing new simulations featuring them.

Tom MU: industry contact · SIM 02 EU: senior advisor · SIM 02 ✓ Consistent

Senior industry professional in both universes. More formal with Maya (cold professional contact), warmer with Ellie (old friend of her father). The same values: specificity over generalities, evidence over claims. One person, two relationships – no conflict.

Claire MU: old acquaintance · SIM 02 EU: hiring manager · SIM 03–04 ✓ Consistent

Brusque with Maya as an old university acquaintance calling in a favour – professional and demanding with Ellie as a hiring manager. Being impatient with a friend asking for contacts doesn't preclude being sharp and competent at work. No conflict.

John MU: mentor · SIM 01 EU: team buddy · SIM 04 ⚠ Monitor

Mentor and former colleague to Maya in SIM 01 – the person the team turns to at Canopy in Ellie Universe. Compatible if the two stories happen at different points in time. No hard conflict – but IDs should align on who John is before writing new simulations featuring him in either universe. If John is used in a new simulation, his role must always be senior enough to mentor someone more junior – while still being approachable enough to be a peer buddy to others.

Alex MU: narrator · all SIMs EU: narrator · all SIMs ✓ Intentional

Same character intentionally across both universes. Narrator and guide – warm, direct, never corporate. Exists outside the story world in both. No conflict and no ambiguity.

04 · Learning objectives by simulation
SIM 01 · Maya's Dilemma
  • Recognize when outside pressure is shaping a career decision
  • Distinguish genuine interest from reaction against expectations
  • Identify when discouraging advice is shutting down reflection
  • Recognize that reassurance alone does not create clarity
  • Question assumptions being made about a new career path
  • Recognize the value of focusing on the next step, not the whole picture
SIM 02 · Maya's Network
  • Recognize networking as a route to the hidden job market
  • Identify when a professional contact is open to a conversation
  • Recognize how to ask for something specific rather than general help
  • Identify how to manage a contact who is reluctant to engage
  • Recognize how to open and close a time-limited professional conversation
  • Identify the questions that move a professional conversation forward
SIM 03 · Maya's Unexpected Call
  • Recognize how to handle an unexpected call from a recruiter
  • Identify what information to gather during a recruiter conversation
  • Recognize how to manage the pace of a recruiter call
  • Identify how to close a recruiter call without overcommitting
  • Recognize when to ask for time before agreeing to next steps
  • Identify what makes a recruiter conversation productive
SIM 04 · Maya's Virtual Interview
  • Recognize how to manage an environmental disruption at the start of a call
  • Identify how to handle it when the interviewer asks you to repeat yourself
  • Recognize how to respond when audio drops mid-question
  • Identify how to handle a screen share that hasn't come through
  • Recognize how to respond to an unexpected question you're not prepared for
  • Identify how to handle it when the interviewer goes silent or freezes
SIM 05 · Maya's Panel Interview
  • Recognize how to open a panel interview with composure
  • Identify how to structure a STAR answer under pressure
  • Recognize how to manage an unexpected interviewer entering the room
  • Identify how to hold your position when challenged on an answer
  • Recognize how to close a panel interview professionally
  • Identify when to ask for clarification on a panel question
SIM 06 · Maya's Night Before
  • Recognize the value of preparation and rest before a first day
  • Identify practical steps that reduce first-day uncertainty
  • Recognize the difference between useful preparation and avoidance
  • Identify how social pressure can affect good decision-making
  • Recognize what it means to be mentally ready to start something new
  • Identify the difference between being prepared and being anxious
SIM 07 · Maya's First Day
  • Recognize how to raise a personal issue that affects your work on day one
  • Identify how to admit you don't know something and ask for help professionally
  • Recognize how to engage with a colleague's briefing without undermining their expertise
  • Identify how to give an honest account of your experience when asked directly
  • Recognize how to tell your manager about a mistake you made
  • Identify how to respond when your manager changes the plan unexpectedly
